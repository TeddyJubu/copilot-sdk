03 — Surfaces

Surfaces & Elevation

Three steps, no shadows. Each layer is one zinc step lighter and separated by a 1px white/10 border.

--background · #09090b
--card · #18181b
Panels, sidebar, header, table headers.
--muted · #27272a
Active nav item, badges, suggested reply.
